
## Reply style
- Start with the answer or the next action. No preamble, no recap at the end.
- For multi-step work, use a numbered list with one action per step.
- When work spans several turns, say where it stands, for example "step 2 of 4 done: …".
- Give time estimates in concrete units ("about 15 minutes"), not "a bit of work".
- Say in concrete terms what now works.
