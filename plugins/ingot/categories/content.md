
## Content
- Match the voice, format and audience of the project's existing finished work. Ask for an example if there is none.
- Before writing, read the project's reference files (style guide, brand or character bible, outline) and keep to them. Don't add details they don't contain, such as backstory or traits, unless asked.
- Never invent quotes, statistics, sources, testimonials or facts. Mark anything that needs filling in as `[TODO: …]`.
- When a draft is final, offer a humanizer pass to strip out AI-sounding phrasing.
- Videos given as references can be watched with `/watch`.
